Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kyrios Homes For Rent. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our application, we may collect:

  • Personal information: Name, email address, phone number and account information.
  • Property information: Preferred location, rental budget, property type, bedroom requirements, property enquiries and housing requests.
  • Location information: Device location, where you grant permission, to help identify relevant properties.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, IP address, advertising identifiers and technical information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display available rental properties.
  • Help users find suitable accommodation.
  • Process property enquiries and housing requests.
  • Connect prospective tenants with property administrators or relevant contacts.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve application security and performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements where applicable.

4. Location Permissions

Our application may request access to your device location to help identify properties relevant to your area. Location access is subject to your device permissions. You can manage or withdraw location permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Our application may use Supabase and its associated infrastructure to store and manage user information. We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Kyrios Homes For Rent may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device information and advertising identifiers to provide, measure and personalize advertisements where permitted.

Users may be presented with consent choices where required by applicable privacy laws.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. We may share necessary information with authorized property administrators, service providers, or relevant authorities where required by law or necessary to protect our users and services.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage accounts, resolve enquiries, meet legal obligations and protect the application. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent or object to certain data processing activities where applicable.

10. Account Deletion

Users who wish to delete their accounts or request removal of personal information may contact us using the privacy contact details provided below. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

Our application is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law.

12. International Users

Our services may be accessed from different countries. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including relevant Nigerian data protection requirements and applicable international privacy regulations.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
