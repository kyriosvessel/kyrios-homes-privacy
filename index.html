1. Introduction

Welcome to Kyrios Homes For Rent. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our application, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal information: Name, email address, phone number and account information.
  • Property information: Preferred property location, rental budget, property type, bedroom requirements, property enquiries and housing requests.
  • Location information: Device location, where you grant permission, to help identify properties relevant to your area.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, IP address, advertising identifiers and technical information.
  • Communications: Messages, enquiries and information you submit through the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display available rental properties.
  • Help users find suitable accommodation.
  • Process property enquiries and housing requests.
  • Connect prospective tenants with property administrators or relevant contacts.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Improve application performance and security.
  • Display and measure advertisements where applicable.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities.

4. Location Permissions

Our application may request access to your device location to help identify properties relevant to your area. Location access is subject to your device permissions. You can manage or withdraw location permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Our application uses or may use Supabase and its associated infrastructure to store and manage user information. We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Kyrios Homes For Rent may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, including device information, IP addresses and advertising identifiers, to provide, measure and personalize advertisements where permitted by applicable law and user consent choices.

Users may be presented with consent choices where required by applicable privacy laws.

Google Privacy Policy

Our application may also rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, database management and technical operations. These providers process information according to their applicable privacy policies and agreements.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may share necessary information:

  • With authorized administrators responsible for managing property enquiries.
  • With service providers supporting our application.
  • When required by law or lawful government requests.
  • When necessary to protect our users, platform or legal rights.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage accounts, resolve enquiries, meet legal obligations and protect the application.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain forms of data processing.
  • Request information about how your personal information is used.

You may contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Account Deletion

Users who wish to delete their accounts or request the removal of their personal information may contact us using the email address provided below.

Please include your account email address and a clear account deletion request. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

Our application is not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization where required by law.

If we discover that such information has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. International Users

Our services may be accessed from different countries. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws, including relevant Nigerian data protection requirements and applicable international privacy regulations.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
